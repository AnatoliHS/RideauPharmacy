Who We Are

Located in the heart of Ottawa at the corner of Rideau Street and King Edward Avenue, I.D.A. Rideau Pharmacy is a proud, locally operated community pharmacy. We believe that your pharmacist should be your most accessible healthcare provider.

Whether you live in the downtown core, work nearby, or are a student in the area, our mission is to provide you with fast, personalized, and comprehensive healthcare without the "big box" wait times.

Our Philosophy

Healthcare shouldn't be a hassle. That’s why we’ve built a pharmacy that goes beyond just counting pills. With an on-site telemedicine walk-in clinic and the ability to prescribe medications directly for minor ailments, we are designed to be your first stop for health and wellness.

From custom medication compounding to offering services in both English and Arabic, we adapt to the unique needs of our diverse Ottawa community.

Why Choose Rideau Pharmacy?

Prescribing Pharmacists

Skip the clinic wait. We can assess and prescribe treatments for minor ailments like pink eye, rashes, UTIs, and insect bites right at the counter.

On-Site Walk-In Clinic

Need a doctor? Access our enhanced telemedicine clinic directly from the pharmacy for quick and easy consultations.

Modern Convenience
