Frequently Asked Questions

How do I transfer my prescriptions to Rideau Pharmacy?

It's incredibly easy! Simply give us a call at (613) 789-4444 or bring in your current medication bottles. Our team will handle the rest by contacting your previous pharmacy to transfer your files seamlessly.

Do you offer prescription delivery?

Yes, we offer free local delivery throughout the Ottawa area. Just let us know when you request your refill, and we will bring your medications straight to your door.

Can the pharmacist prescribe medications?

Our prescribing pharmacists can assess and write prescriptions for a variety of minor ailments right at the counter, including pink eye, UTIs, cold sores, and insect bites.

How does the on-site telemedicine clinic work?

Consult with a licensed physician via a secure video link from our pharmacy. The doctor can diagnose conditions and send prescriptions straight to our pharmacists for immediate pickup.

Do you provide compliance blister packaging?

Absolutely. We offer custom blister packaging to help you organize your daily doses by day and time, ensuring you take the right medication exactly when needed.

What languages are spoken at Rideau Pharmacy?

Our friendly team is fluent and ready to assist you in English, French, and Arabic.
