Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our services, prescriptions, and clinic. If you don't find your answer here, feel free to reach out.

How do I transfer my prescriptions to Rideau Pharmacy?

It's incredibly easy! Simply give us a call at (613) 789-4444 or bring in your current medication bottles or prescription receipts. Our team will handle the rest by contacting your previous pharmacy to transfer your files seamlessly without any interruption to your care.

Do you offer prescription delivery?

Yes, we offer free local delivery throughout the Ottawa area. Whether you have limited mobility, a busy schedule, or are feeling unwell, just let us know when you request your refill, and we will bring your medications straight to your door.

Can the pharmacist prescribe medications, or do I always need a doctor?

Our prescribing pharmacists can assess and write prescriptions for a variety of minor ailments right at the counter. This includes common issues like pink eye, uncomplicated UTIs, skin rashes, cold sores, and insect bites. It’s a great way to skip the clinic wait for minor health concerns.

How does the on-site telemedicine walk-in clinic work?

Our on-site clinic allows you to consult with a licensed physician via a secure, private video link directly from our pharmacy. The doctor can diagnose conditions, recommend treatments, and immediately send any necessary prescriptions straight to our pharmacists so you can leave with your medication in hand.

I take multiple medications. Do you provide compliance blister packaging?

Absolutely. We offer custom blister packaging to help you organize your daily doses by day and time. This takes the guesswork out of managing multiple prescriptions and ensures you are taking the right medication exactly when you need it.

What languages are spoken at Rideau Pharmacy?

To best serve our diverse Ottawa community, our friendly team is fluent and ready to assist you in English, French, and Arabic.
